# Auphelie privacy policy

Draft for review — prepared 30 October 2026. Not published or effective.

Auphelie is a personal audiobook player published by Michael Kopt. You choose your own local files or connect to an Audiobookshelf server. Auphelie does not supply audiobooks or operate an audiobook hosting service.

## Information on your device

When you add a local library, Auphelie accesses the folders and audio files you select and reads book information such as titles, tracks, durations and cover images. It stores library references, settings, playback sessions, listening history and progress so you can return to your books. Metadata, covers and downloaded server media may be cached in app storage. Local playback does not require an Auphelie account or sending your local audiobook collection to the publisher.

## Connecting to Audiobookshelf

Connecting is optional. The app sends connection requests and your login details to the HTTPS server you specify. Subsequent requests use an access token and any custom headers you configure. The server receives requests for libraries, books, artwork and playback, together with listening progress updates such as book identifiers, position and completion. Its operator can also observe connection information such as your IP address and request times.

Auphelie saves connection metadata locally and uses platform secure storage for passwords, access tokens and custom headers. HTTPS protects supported server traffic in transit; this is not a promise of end-to-end encryption or a guarantee of a server operator's security.

Your server operator controls its accounts, content, logs, backups, retention and use of information it receives. Auphelie cannot specify a universal retention period or delete those records on your behalf. Consult that operator's privacy information before connecting, especially to a server run by someone else.

## Diagnostics, advertising and tracking

The intended standard store Release does not enable the optional development telemetry instrumentation. This draft proposes no advertising, cross-service tracking or sale of personal data. Diagnostic or telemetry-enabled builds need separate disclosure before distribution; this policy draft must not be taken as a description of every development build. Device operating systems and app stores may separately process diagnostics under their own settings and policies.

## Support correspondence

If you email michael.kopt@outlook.cz, Michael Kopt receives your email address, message and any attachments you send. The email provider also processes that correspondence to deliver and store it. We use your message to investigate and respond to your request. Do not send passwords, tokens, custom authentication headers or private book files. A fixed mailbox or backup retention period has not been established for this draft. You may use the same address to request deletion of support correspondence; any applicable retention limitations must be explained when handling that request.

## Your choices and deletion

You can use local libraries without connecting a server. You can remove library sources and disconnect Audiobookshelf. Disconnecting clears the stored connection secrets and associated server sources; it does not delete the server account or promise removal of server-side progress and logs. Removing a library is not a promise to erase every local history record, cache or original book file. Device app-storage controls may let you clear local app data. Platform backup and secure-storage behavior can differ; deletion of all backups or credentials after uninstall is not guaranteed. Ask the server operator about deleting its records and contact the publisher about support messages.

## Children and content

The proposed audience is general teen and adult listeners. Auphelie does not curate a children's catalog, filter book content or verify listener age. Books, artwork and metadata supplied by you or your server can contain mature material. Choose libraries appropriate for the listener.

## Changes and contact

Review this page for changes when app data practices change. A publication/effective date will be added only after approval and deployment. For questions about Auphelie privacy or support correspondence, contact Michael Kopt at michael.kopt@outlook.cz. For a connected server's data, contact its operator.
